Troubleshooting

Game won't load or crashes on launch: Delete and reinstall the app. Note that your progress is saved on your device only, so reinstalling starts a new profile.

Lost save data: Game progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress with it, and it cannot be recovered.

Grandma's tips: If you skipped a tip or want to see them again, turn on Grandma's tips in Settings.

Purchases: One Last Strike is a paid app with no in-app purchases and no ads. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

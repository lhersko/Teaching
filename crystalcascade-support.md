Troubleshooting

Game won't load or crashes on launch: Delete and reinstall the app. If iCloud is on, your progress is restored from iCloud when you open the game again.

Lost save data: Game progress is saved on your device and, when you are signed in to iCloud, synced through iCloud. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account on both devices. Resetting progress from Settings inside the game cannot be undone.

Purchases: Gem packs and bundles are delivered as soon as Apple confirms the payment. Bundles bought once, such as the Starter Bundle, come back automatically after a reinstall or on a new device; you can also tap Restore purchases in the shop or in Settings. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

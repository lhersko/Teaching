Troubleshooting

Game won't load or crashes on launch: Delete and reinstall the app. On iPhone and iPad, your purchase of the full game stays with your Apple Account: in the game's Settings, open Full game and tap Restore purchase. On Android, the game stays with the Google Account that bought it: install it again from Google Play.

Lost save data: Game progress is saved on your device, and a copy is kept in your iCloud account (iPhone and iPad) or in Google Play Games saved games (Android) when you are signed in. On a new device, sign in with the same account and open the game: it offers the saved game. Resetting progress in the game's settings deletes your progress but never your purchase.

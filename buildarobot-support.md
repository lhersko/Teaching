Troubleshooting

Game won't load or crashes on launch: Delete and reinstall the app. Your purchase of the full game stays with your Apple Account or Google Account: in the game's Settings, open Full game and tap Restore purchase.

Lost save data: Game progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it. Resetting progress in the game's settings deletes your progress but never your purchase.

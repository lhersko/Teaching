Troubleshooting

Game won't load or crashes on launch: Delete and reinstall the app. If iCloud is on, your progress is restored from iCloud when you open the game again.

Lost save data: Game progress is saved on your device and, when you are signed in to iCloud, synced through iCloud. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account on both devices. A save you delete from inside the game cannot be recovered.

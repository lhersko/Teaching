# Privacy Policy: Digline (Android)

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Digline is a single-player game with no account sign-up, no ads and no user tracking. It does collect a small amount of anonymous technical and usage information, described in full below.

## Who is responsible for your data

The data controller for Digline is Luis Herskovic, an independent developer based in Chile. You can reach me at lstmnspprt@gmail.com about anything on this page, including any request about your data, and I will always answer.

## What is collected, and why

- **Google (Firebase Analytics)** — Approximate usage data: which screens you open, which in-game actions you take, session length, app version, device model, operating system version, language and country of your Google Play storefront. Purpose: To understand which parts of the game people play and where they get stuck, so the game can be improved.
- **Google (Firebase Analytics)** — A randomly generated app instance identifier. This is created by the app on your device, is not your Google account, is not the advertising ID, and is reset if you delete and reinstall the app. This app does not collect the advertising ID at all: collection is switched off in the build and the permission is removed. Purpose: To count distinct installs so usage statistics are not double-counted.

None of this is linked to your real-world identity. I cannot identify an individual person from any of it.

## What is never collected

- No name, email address, phone number or postal address.
- No advertising ID and no advertising SDKs. The app does not request the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission.
- No location data. The app never requests location permission and contains no location code.
- No contacts, photos, microphone, camera or health data.
- No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and nothing is sold or shared with data brokers.
- No crash or error reporting. This build contains no crash-reporting library of any kind.
- No Google Play Games Services. This build is not signed in to Play Games and submits no achievements or leaderboard scores.
- No in-app purchases and no payment information. There is nothing to buy in this game.

## Legal basis (UK/EU GDPR)

Where the UK or EU GDPR applies, the legal basis for the processing above is my legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) in keeping the game stable and understanding how it is used, balanced against your privacy by keeping the data anonymous, minimal and free of any advertising or cross-app tracking.

You can object to this processing at any time by contacting me at lstmnspprt@gmail.com, or simply by deleting the app.

## Where your game data lives

Your game progress is saved locally on your device, in the app's own private storage. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

There is no cloud save in this version. Your progress lives only on this device, so it does not follow you to a new phone.

## How long it is kept

Analytics records are retained for up to 14 months and are then deleted automatically by Google.

## Your rights

If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area, or another region with comparable law, you have the right to request access to your data, correction, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, and to object to processing. Because the data collected is anonymous, I usually cannot connect it to you individually, but if you contact me I will do whatever is technically possible, and I will always answer.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection supervisory authority. In the EU you can find yours at https://edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/board/members_en ; in the UK it is the Information Commissioner's Office at https://ico.org.uk .

## California residents

I do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act, and I have never done so. There are no financial incentives tied to data, and you will never be treated differently in the game for exercising a privacy right.

## Children

Digline is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them. No age is requested and no profile is built. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact me and I will delete whatever can be identified.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

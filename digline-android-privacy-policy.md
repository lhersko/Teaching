# Privacy Policy: Digline (Android)

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Digline is a mining game with no ads or advertising tracking. It uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for usage information and diagnostics. Android version 1.0.17 adds Google Play Games features described below; signing in uses your existing Google Play Games profile.

## Who is responsible for your data

The data controller for Digline is Luis Herskovic, an independent developer based in Chile. You can reach me at lstmnspprt@gmail.com about anything on this page, including any request about your data, and I will always answer.

## What is collected, and why

- **Google (Firebase Analytics)** — Approximate usage data: which screens you open, which in-game actions you take, session length, app version, device model, operating system version, language and country of your Google Play storefront. Purpose: To understand which parts of the game people play and where they get stuck, so the game can be improved.
- **Google (Firebase Analytics)** — A randomly generated app instance identifier. This is created by the app on your device, is not your Google account, is not the advertising ID, and is reset if you delete and reinstall the app. This app does not collect the advertising ID at all: collection is switched off in the build and the permission is removed. Purpose: To count distinct installs so usage statistics are not double-counted.
- **Google (Firebase Crashlytics)** — Crash and error diagnostics: the crash stack trace, device model, operating system version, app version, free memory and storage at the time of the crash, and a randomly generated installation identifier. Purpose: To find and fix crashes and performance problems.

The Firebase usage and diagnostic information described above is separate from the Play Games profile used for achievements, scores and cloud progress.

## Google Play Games

Starting with Android version 1.0.17, Digline uses Google Play Games Services for game-specific player sign-in, achievements, Daily Dig rankings and cloud progress. Earlier Android versions do not contain these features. Google handles your gamer identity, achievements, submitted scores and cloud player-save data. Digline reads your game-specific player identifier and leaderboard display names; leaderboard visibility follows your Play Games profile settings. The SDK also collects usage and diagnostic information. Game data travels over HTTPS. The game does not request Google Account email, a friends list, or access to your ordinary Drive files.

## What is never collected

- No personal contact details such as Google Account email, phone number or postal address are requested by the game. Play Games gamer identifiers and leaderboard display names are used for game features.
- No advertising ID and no advertising SDKs. The app does not request the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission.
- No location data. The app never requests location permission and contains no location code.
- No contacts, photos, microphone, camera or health data.
- No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and nothing is sold or shared with data brokers.
- No in-app purchases and no payment information. There is nothing to buy in this game.

## Legal basis (UK/EU GDPR)

Where the UK or EU GDPR applies, the legal basis for the Firebase usage and diagnostic processing is my legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) in keeping the game stable and understanding how it is used, balanced against your privacy by keeping the data anonymous, minimal and free of any advertising or cross-app tracking.

You can object to this processing at any time by contacting me at lstmnspprt@gmail.com, or simply by deleting the app.

## Where your game data lives

Your game progress is saved locally on your device, in the app's own private storage. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Your progress is saved locally first. In Android 1.0.17 and later, when connected to Google Play Games, core player progress can also sync through Saved Games in this game's application-data storage in your Google account. This includes inventory, equipment, home and world progression. Terrain chunks, active runs, separate Pay Dirt history, device preferences and local Daily Dig records do not transfer. Restoring cloud progress requires an in-game choice and confirmation; terrain is regenerated. A normal app update preserves existing local saves. You can manage or delete this game's Google-held data through your Play Games profile or Google Account. Deleting cloud data does not itself erase local progress; uninstalling removes local app data.

## How long it is kept

Analytics records are retained for up to 14 months and are then deleted automatically by Google. Crash reports are retained for up to 90 days.

## Your rights

If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area, or another region with comparable law, you have the right to request access to your data, correction, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, and to object to processing. Because Firebase usage and diagnostic information is not collected under your personal contact details, I usually cannot connect those records to you individually, but if you contact me I will do whatever is technically possible, and I will always answer.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection supervisory authority. In the EU you can find yours at https://edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/board/members_en ; in the UK it is the Information Commissioner's Office at https://ico.org.uk .

## California residents

I do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act, and I have never done so. There are no financial incentives tied to data, and you will never be treated differently in the game for exercising a privacy right.

## Children

Digline is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The game does not request your age or create its own personal account; Play Games features use an existing Google profile. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact me and I will delete whatever can be identified.

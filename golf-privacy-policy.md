# Privacy Policy: Last Putt

Last updated: 02 August 2026

Last Putt is a single-player game. It collects no data at all: no analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts and no ads. Everything the game stores stays on your device.

## Who is responsible for your data

The data controller for Last Putt is Luis Herskovic, Camino Punta de Aguilas 9300, Santiago, Región Metropolitana, Chile. You can reach me at lstmnspprt@gmail.com about anything on this page.

## What is never collected

- No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting of any kind. This game contains no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK.
- No name, email address, phone number or postal address.
- No advertising identifier (IDFA) and no advertising SDKs.
- No location data. The app never requests location permission and contains no location code.
- No contacts, photos, microphone, camera or health data.
- No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and nothing is sold or shared with data brokers.

## Where your game data lives

Your game progress is saved locally on your device.

## Children

Last Putt is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them. No age is requested and no profile is built. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact me and I will delete whatever can be identified.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
